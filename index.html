About

I am an Associate Professor in Finance at Macquarie University Business School. My research focuses on fintech, decentralized finance, corporate culture, and economic history, and has appeared in journals including the Review of Finance, Research Policy, and the Journal of Corporate Finance.

I received my PhD in Finance from the University of Queensland, an MPhil in Statistics from Renmin University of China, and a BA in Statistics from Tongji University.
